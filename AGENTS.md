# Agent notes (united-utilities-hass)

- Thin HA integration over the `united-utilities` PyPI library. API, auth and
  parsing changes belong in `united-utilities-py`, not here.
- **Never retry a rejected PIN.** United Utilities locks the account after 3
  wrong PINs (HTTP 423, about an hour). `AuthError` sets `auth_failed` in the entry data and raises
  `ConfigEntryAuthFailed`; setup refuses to log in while the flag is set, so HA
  restarts don't spend strikes. Only a successful reauth clears it.
- The config flow looks up the customer ID from account number + postcode
  (`lookup_customer_id`). The postcode is PII and is never stored, logged or put
  in entry data.
- An expired token (`NotLoggedInError`) gets exactly one fresh login per poll.
- The library pin is exact and lives in two places: `manifest.json`
  `requirements` and `requirements_test.txt`. Bump both together (CI checks).
- `translations/en.json` is a copy of `strings.json`; edit `strings.json` and
  copy it over (pre-commit and CI check).
- Diagnostics are allowlist-built. Never add credentials, account number or the
  meter serial to diagnostics, attributes, device names or entity names.
- Tests mock the client class; no test may reach the real API.
- Conventional commits drive release-please; don't hand-edit versions.
- Brand PNGs are generated by `scripts/make_brand_assets.py` from the SVGs in
  `scripts/brand_src/` (converted from the app's vector logo). Edit those, not
  the PNGs. They live outside `custom_components/` so they don't ship in the
  HACS zip.
- Bill PDFs are fetched for tariffs. Only a `trustworthy` bill (no `Bill.problems`)
  feeds the price sensors; otherwise they go unavailable and a `bill_unreadable`
  repair issue lists the problems. Never log, store or put bill PDFs or their
  text in diagnostics: they contain the holder's name, address and bank details.
